Questions

Good to know.

What documents do I need to start?

Usually the sale deed or title document, link documents, encumbrance certificate, tax receipts and a site survey. We share an exact checklist for your project after the first consultation.

Which buildings need a Fire NOC?

Generally high-rise buildings and larger commercial, institutional, assembly and industrial buildings, depending on height and built-up area. We check what applies to your building and handle both the provisional and final NOC.

What is the difference between DTCP and HMDA approval?

HMDA covers the Hyderabad Metropolitan Development Area, while DTCP covers layouts and developments in other areas of the state. We work out which authority has jurisdiction over your land and apply there.

Do I need RERA registration?

Most real estate projects above the prescribed plot area or number of units must be registered before marketing or sale, and so must real estate agents. We handle registration and the ongoing filings.

How long does an approval take?

It depends on the authority and type of permission. A typical house permission is quicker, while layouts and Fire NOCs take longer. You get a realistic timeline before we begin.
