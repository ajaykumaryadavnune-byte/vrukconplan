Telangana Approvals

Permission consultancy, clearly coordinated

We help clients understand the likely approval route, organise technical documents and coordinate applications with the competent authority. The authority decides and issues statutory approvals.

GHMC & Municipalities

Building permission documentation, revised plans and applicable completion/occupancy application support.

HMDA & DTCP

Layout planning, land development documentation and applicable layout approval application coordination.

Gram Panchayat & UDA

Jurisdiction checks and building/layout application support under the applicable rules.

Schools & Hospitals

Project planning and coordination of applicable building and departmental clearances.

Poultry, Industrial & Warehousing

Site feasibility, drawings, estimates and coordination of relevant local and departmental permissions.

Special NOCs

Fire, irrigation/FTL, environmental, pollution consent and airport height clearance coordination where applicable.

Regulatory note: Requirements depend on site location, land use, plot dimensions, road width, building height, project category and current rules. Services are subject to professional authorisation and competent authority jurisdiction.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can VRUKSHA CONPLAN help with building permissions?

Yes. We provide technical documentation and application coordination for applicable projects. The competent authority reviews and issues the statutory approval.

Do you provide structural design services?

Structural consultancy includes RCC, steel, foundation and analysis services, subject to project scope and qualified professional review.

Which areas do you serve?

We aim to serve projects across Telangana. Specific availability and competent authority jurisdiction are confirmed during the initial review.

Can you provide a project estimate?

Yes. We can prepare item-wise estimates, BOQ, material and labour analysis or a project quotation after reviewing the project details.
